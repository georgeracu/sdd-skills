# Pitch: Invoice PDFs

**Appetite:** two weeks

## Problem

Finance teams have to copy Invoice lines out of the Dashboard into their own systems. They want a PDF of each Invoice.

## Solution

Billing renders a PDF of each Invoice when the Billing_Period closes and stores it. The Dashboard shows a download link on the Invoice page.

## No-gos

- The PDF is not emailed. Nothing changes in Notifications.
- No change to how usage is counted.
